
Kanban is a useful plugin that I use a lot and that completely replaced my notes containing an infinity of to do list of `[ x ]`.  The idea is to mimic a **board** when you stick some post-it (called **cards**) that you can drag through different categories depending on their state of advancement. An example of use of Kanban is available in [[📌 KANBAN - TO DO LIST]].

# SOME COOL TIPS...

## Create a note associated with a task 
One cool feature of Kanban is that you can create a note associated with your **card**. It allows to be more details in what you have to do or what you have done while keeping readability on the board. In order to create a note from a card, you need to to click on the three little dots on the top of your cards, and click on "new note from this card". 

The created notes will automatically go in the folder "Kanban notes" (you can change that in the Kanban settings). To this day, I don't know how to automatically erase a note associated with a removed cards. If someone discovers how to do that please let me know...

## Set date to visualise to keep track
`Kanban` enables to add a **date** on your cards. To do this, you only have to insert a `@` within a card, and it will open a calendar where you'll have to chose a date for your task. Something cool (at least in my opinion...) is that you set a bunch of colors in Kanban settings depending on how far is the date (which I'm supposed is a deadline right) from today. Note that it will adjust automatically with time passing of course :

For this Vault, I set that the date appears in **green** as long as it more than 2 weeks before the deadline, **orange** when it's within 2 weeks before the deadline, **light red** when it's within 1 week before the deadline, and **red** when the deadline is passed (see above).
